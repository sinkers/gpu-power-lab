Why it matters

A GPU fleet that can be driven to its ceiling and then modulated is a controllable load. The same capability, uncontrolled, is a power-quality problem.

The trap we nearly fell into

The obvious way to write the tensor loop draws 460 W. The right way draws 989 W. Same silicon, same session — a 528 W measurement error waiting to happen.

What real jobs leave on the table

Training and inference sit 10–30% below this ceiling. That gap is overhead — sync barriers, collective waits, memory stalls — not silicon.
